Privacy Policy

This is a convenience translation of PapaMap's German privacy policy. In case of any discrepancy, the German version is authoritative.

Controller

Jakub Waller, c/o IP-Management #11204, Ludwig-Erhard-Str. 18, 20459 Hamburg, Germany, email: papamap@jakubwaller.eu

Principle

This website is a static map: no accounts, no forms, no analytics or tracking cookies, no server-side database of visitor data.

Hosting / server logs

The website is hosted on a privately operated server. Accessing it processes technically necessary data (IP address, timestamp, requested address). Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Cloudflare (delivery & protection)

Delivery runs through Cloudflare, Inc. (content delivery & DDoS protection). Cloudflare processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver and protect the site, and may set a technically necessary anti-bot cookie — no profiling, no consent required. Cloudflare is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; a data processing agreement (DPA) is in place.

Map data (OpenStreetMap)

The map loads map tiles from tile.openstreetmap.org; this transmits your IP address to the OpenStreetMap Foundation (OSMF Privacy Policy). The map software (MapLibre) is served from this server, not from a third party.

Location (locating feature)

The two locate buttons request your location through the browser's Geolocation interface — only after your explicit permission, which you can revoke at any time. Your position is processed exclusively in your browser: the search for the nearest usable changing table runs against the map data that has already been loaded anyway. Your location is not transmitted to this server or to any third party, not stored and not logged. The “Directions” button opens a link with the changing table's coordinates: on Android a geo: link — which map app receives it is decided by your device —, on an iPhone or iPad Apple Maps (maps.apple.com), on every other device the directions page of openstreetmap.org in a new tab. Your own location is not part of that link; once it is open, the privacy terms of that provider apply.

If you have shared your location through one of the two buttons and a changing table whose room nobody has recorded yet lies no more than 75 m away, a small note at the bottom edge asks whether you know the room. For this the site goes on using the last position it determined for a few minutes; it lives only in the open page's working memory and is neither stored nor transmitted. If you close the note, the browser remembers in local storage (key papamap-card-dismissed) for which places it should no longer appear — purely functional, only on your device (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

Language setting (local storage)

If you choose a language via the language switcher, that choice is stored in your browser's local storage (key papamap-lang); the same applies to whether you read the map from a dad's or a mum's point of view (key papamap-mode) and whether the "Wheelchair" filter is on (key papamap-wheelchair) — purely functional, without tracking, only on your device, deletable via your browser data (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, Germany's Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act).

Offline storage (service worker)

So the map also works without a network connection, a service worker stores the page itself and the changing-table data in your browser's cache — purely functional, without tracking, only on your device, deletable via your browser data (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). No data is transmitted to this server or to any third party in the process. Map tiles are explicitly not stored — tile.openstreetmap.org's terms of use prohibit that. Without a network connection, the map therefore shows the stored changing tables with no map background.

Links to OSM editors

Links to MapComplete, StreetComplete and openstreetmap.org lead to external services; there you edit data under your own OSM account, subject to their privacy policies — nothing about this runs through this website.

Answering on the map: signing in to OpenStreetMap (OAuth 2)

If you tap one of the room buttons in a grey pin, you are redirected once to openstreetmap.org and sign in there with your OSM account (OAuth 2 with PKCE; this website never sees your password). OSM returns an access token to the site, which is kept in your browser's local storage (key papamap-osm-token, plus your OSM display name under papamap-osm-user), until you tap "Log out" in the pin or delete your browser data (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). Your answer is written by the browser directly to api.openstreetmap.org, as a changeset under your own OSM account and name, marked with created_by=PapaMap; the OSMF Privacy Policy applies. This server learns nothing of it: the token, the account and the answer never pass through papamap.de, and PapaMap stores no edits. The map remains fully usable without signing in.

Checking back after an edit (OSM API)

If you tap "Answer on MapComplete" in a pin, the site queries api.openstreetmap.org for that one object's current tags a few times over the following minutes, to show you your change. This transmits your IP address to the OpenStreetMap Foundation (see above, OSMF Privacy Policy); your OSM account is not involved. Which object is being checked is remembered by the browser in session storage (key papamap-edit-check) and discarded when the tab is closed — purely functional, only on your device (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

My PapaMap: your own answers and saved places

The "My PapaMap" dialog shows how many answers you have given yourself. If you are signed in to OpenStreetMap, the site requests the public list of your own changesets from api.openstreetmap.org when the dialog opens — the same list anyone can see in your OSM profile — and counts, on your device, those made through PapaMap or through MapComplete's PapaMap theme. The request names your OSM display name but sends no access token; it transmits your IP address to the OpenStreetMap Foundation (see above). The number, position and date of these changesets are kept by the browser in local storage (key papamap-my-answers) so they need not be loaded again each time; they are deleted when you log out. This server learns nothing of it, and PapaMap compares you with nobody: there is no ranking of people.

The star in a pin saves a place. The list (OSM link, the place's name and coordinates, the time) is kept exclusively in your browser's local storage (key papamap-saved) until you remove the place in the dialog or delete your browser data. Purely functional, only on your device (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

Sharing a place

The share button in a pin hands the place's name and a link to papamap.de to your device's share function. Which app or person the link goes to is entirely your decision; that app's own terms apply to it. Where the device offers no share function, or it fails, the site copies the link to the clipboard. Whoever opens such a link requests papamap.de with ?osm=… appended; it names only the place and, like any requested address, appears in the server log (see above).

The app (iPhone, iPad)

PapaMap is also available as an app, distributed through the App Store and through TestFlight. The app is the same map in a native shell. The same principle applies: no account with us, no analytics, tracking or advertising components, no crash-reporting system of our own, no push notifications. The app itself talks to two servers — papamap.de for the changing-table data and the offline cities, openstreetmap.org for map tiles and for your answers. On top of that comes whatever you open through a link, and Apple's role in distribution; both are covered further below. The following sections describe what is different or additional in the app; otherwise the above applies accordingly.

App: data from papamap.de

On startup, the app loads the changing-table data from papamap.de and keeps a copy in the app's own data directory, so the map shows pins even without a network connection. These are ordinary requests for fixed files; the server sees the same thing as during a website visit (see "Hosting / server logs" and "Cloudflare" above). The requests contain no information about you personally and no location. The copy stays only on your device — apart from a device backup (iCloud or computer), if you have set one up (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

App: map data and offline cities

The app, too, loads map tiles from tile.openstreetmap.org; this transmits your IP address to the OpenStreetMap Foundation (see above). The tiles loaded are those of the map section currently shown — that is, the section you are looking at, not your coordinates. In addition, you can use "Save a city offline" to download a city's map section to your device. The file comes from papamap.de; as with any file request, the request tells the server which city was chosen. Which cities are saved is remembered by the app on the device (key papamap-offline-cities). A saved city is drawn by the app from the file on the device; without a network connection, no tiles at all are requested. "Delete" in the same dialog removes a city again.

App: location

The locate button, the search for the nearest changing table, the Siri Shortcut, the home-screen widget and the "Nearest changing table" control in Control Center (also on the Lock Screen or the Action button) request your location through iOS — only after your explicit permission ("While Using the App"), which you can revoke at any time in iOS Settings. Your position is processed exclusively on your device: the search for the nearest usable changing table runs against the data that is already on the device anyway. Your location is not transmitted to this server or to any third party, not stored and not logged — not from the widget, the Shortcut or Control Center either. The widget recalculates the nearest changing table on the device at regular intervals as long as it sits on the home screen or the lock screen. After you tap the locate button, the map shows your surroundings; it then loads the tiles for that section from tile.openstreetmap.org as described above.

App: widget and Siri Shortcut

So that the widget and the Shortcut name the same changing table as the map, the app keeps a shortened copy of the changing-table data (coordinates, status, name, OSM link) as well as your two settings — dad or mum view and language — in a shared area on the device that only the app and its widget can read. Both stay, apart from a device backup (iCloud or computer), on the device (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). Siri and Shortcuts are a feature of iOS; how Apple processes a spoken request is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

App: settings on the device

The app stores the same functional keys as the website — language (papamap-lang), dad or mum view (papamap-mode), the "Wheelchair" filter (papamap-wheelchair) and, after signing in, the access token and your OSM display name (papamap-osm-token, papamap-osm-user), plus the saved places (papamap-saved), your own answers (papamap-my-answers) and the closed room notes (papamap-card-dismissed) — just in the app's own storage instead of the browser's. Purely functional, without tracking, only on your device — and, like everything the app stores, in a device backup (iCloud or computer), if you have set one up (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

App: signing in to OpenStreetMap

Sign-in works as on the website (OAuth 2 with PKCE), with one difference: openstreetmap.org's sign-in page opens the app in the system's in-app browser view, which the app itself cannot see into, and OSM then sends you back into the app via the address papamap://auth. The app never sees your password. Your answer is written by the app directly to api.openstreetmap.org, as a changeset under your own OSM account and name; the OSMF Privacy Policy applies. This server learns nothing of it. The map remains fully usable without signing in.

App: links to other pages

Every link that leaves the map — the pages of this website, openstreetmap.org, MapComplete, StreetComplete — opens in your device's system browser, inside a view within the app. When the app opens a page of this website, it appends ?app=1 to its address; this only indicates that the request comes from the app, and, like any requested address, it appears in the server log (see above). The map and the country and leaderboard pages then remember, for the duration of that view, that they were opened from the app (key papamap-in-app in session storage), and hide the donation link accordingly; the entry expires when you close the view (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). From that point on, the privacy policy of the respective service applies; this policy applies to the pages of this website.

App: Apple (App Store and TestFlight)

Distribution through the App Store and through TestFlight is Apple's responsibility. Apple is independently responsible for this and processes the data involved under its own privacy policy. From the App Store we receive metrics and crash reports only in aggregated form, and only insofar as you have agreed to share them with developers on your device. If you take part in a test through TestFlight, Apple provides us, under its own terms, with crash data and — if you send feedback — that feedback along with the details Apple supplies with it, such as the email address you were invited with. We use this exclusively for troubleshooting (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The app itself sends us none of this.

App: withdrawal and deletion

You withdraw location sharing in iOS Settings under PapaMap; you delete saved cities with "Delete" in the "Save a city offline" dialog; you sign out of OpenStreetMap with "Log out" in the pin. If you delete the app, everything it stored on the device disappears with it: the copy of the changing-table data, the offline cities, the settings, the saved places, the list of your own answers and the access token. Whatever of that lives in a device backup you have set up is managed by iOS; it disappears with that backup. What you have written to OpenStreetMap stays there — that is the whole point.

Contact by email or form

When you contact us by email, the details you provide are processed only to handle your enquiry (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and are not passed on.

The contact form linked from the Impressum is on buergerwecker.de, another website of the same operator; its privacy policy applies to processing there. This website itself collects and stores nothing in the process.

Your rights

Access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), objection (Art. 21 GDPR). Contact: papamap@jakubwaller.eu.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the one where you live.
