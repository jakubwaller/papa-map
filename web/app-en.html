PapaMap as an app

The same map, closer to hand: offline, the nearest table in one tap, and on the iPhone through Siri and a widget too.

How to get PapaMap on your phone

iPhone

With Siri, a widget and city maps offline.

Android

Tell me when it’s out

The app is in development. One e-mail when it’s ready, then the address is deleted.

Without a store

iPhone: the Share icon, then “Add to Home Screen”.

Android: the browser menu, then “Install” or “Add to Home screen”.

Works in every browser, just not from inside Instagram or Bluesky.

Free, no ads, no account of its own.

What the app adds

1 Find

The nearest changing table, one tap.

  • The button at the bottom centre finds the nearest changing table you can actually reach.
  • With the distance as the crow flies, and opening hours where they are known.
  • Dad or mum: set once. As a dad, the map colours tables by whether you can get to them; as a mum, every table counts.

2 On the go

Works without a signal too.

  • The changing tables live on the phone, in the car park, on the train and in the supermarket basement too.
  • The iPhone app also saves a whole city, map underneath included. Hamburg is 27 MB.
  • “Directions” opens your navigation app, for example Apple Maps, Google Maps or Organic Maps.

3 Contribute

Grey pin? Two taps.

  • “Which room is the changing table in?” Your answer goes straight to OpenStreetMap, under your own account there.
  • A place missing? The app lists what OpenStreetMap already knows nearby, and you answer right there.
  • In within a minute, for everyone who comes after you.

4 Your PapaMap

Your city, your answers.

  • My PapaMap shows your answers, the places you saved and how much of your city is answered already.
  • iPhone only: Siri, a widget and a Control Center control show the nearest table without opening the app.

Free, like the map. The data stays OpenStreetMap; the app has none of its own and collects none.
