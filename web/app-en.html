PapaMap as an app — do you want it?

It does not exist yet. Whether it will is decided on this page: two buttons, one counter.

Would you install it?

The tap is only counted: time and button, nothing else. No account, no cookie, no IP address.

Thanks — counted.

One vote for iPhone. That is all it takes; the number decides.

If you want to try the test version as soon as there is one, a short email is enough. The address is used for that and nothing else.

What the app could do

  • Offline. The changing tables of your area live on the phone — in the car park, on the train and in the supermarket basement, where there is no signal.
  • The nearest table, one tap. The app shows the nearest changing table you can actually reach, with the walk to it.
  • Dad or mum. Set once. As a dad, the map keeps colouring tables by whether you can get to them; as a mum, every table counts.
  • Grey pin? Two taps. “Which room is the table in?” — your answer goes straight to OpenStreetMap, with no detour through another site.
  • Add a table where you stand. A missing changing table is in within a minute — for everyone who comes after you.

Free, like the map. The data stays OpenStreetMap; the app would have none of its own and collect none.

No date, no promise

An app in the store costs time and a yearly fee. Both are only worth it if enough people want it — which is why there is no date here, only a counter. If it is many, it comes: first as a test version, then for everyone. If it is few, it does not, and the map stays as it is.
