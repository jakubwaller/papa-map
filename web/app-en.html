Without a store: how to get the map on your phone

iPhone: the Share icon, then “Add to Home Screen”. Works in every browser, just not from inside Instagram or Bluesky.

Android: the browser menu, then “Install” or “Add to Home screen”. Chrome, Firefox, Samsung Internet, all the others too.

What the app does

  • Offline. The changing tables live on the phone, in the car park, on the train and in the supermarket basement too. Only the map underneath needs a signal.
  • The nearest table, one tap. The app shows the nearest changing table you can actually reach, with the distance as the crow flies.
  • Dad or mum. Set once. As a dad, the map colours tables by whether you can get to them; as a mum, every table counts.
  • Grey pin? Two taps. “Which room is the table in?” Your answer goes straight to OpenStreetMap, under your own account there.
  • Add a table where you stand. A missing changing table is in within a minute, for everyone who comes after you.
  • iPhone app only: save a city offline, Hamburg is 27 MB, and the map underneath works without a signal too. Siri, a widget and a Control Center control show the nearest table without opening the app.

Free, like the map. The data stays OpenStreetMap; the app has none of its own and collects none.
