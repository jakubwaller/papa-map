Add a missing place

PapaMap has no data of its own — new places go into OpenStreetMap and show up here after the nightly refresh.

A public toilet is missing Opens MapComplete at this map view — add it and answer the changing-table questions. Needs a free OSM login. A café / shop / restaurant has a table The place almost certainly exists on OSM already — open the editor here and add the changing_table tags. Step-by-step: see Methods.
