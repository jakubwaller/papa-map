This is the last TestFlight build of PapaMap. The app has been on the App Store since 25 September, and that is where it continues: https://apps.apple.com/de/app/papamap/id6813376985
– To switch: delete this TestFlight version first, then install PapaMap from the App Store. Places you saved and cities you downloaded on this phone do not come along; your answers are safe, they live in OpenStreetMap.
– Since your 1.0 build the app has learned a lot: your answers show on the map within minutes, places with opening hours say "Open now", there is a "Wheelchair" chip and a "High chair" chip, "+ Add a place" lists the cafés around the map centre that have no changing table on the map yet, a welcome screen on first launch, and "My PapaMap" with your area, your answers, your saved places and a Feedback line.
– Thank you for testing. This TestFlight group stays open for a while so I can see that this note arrived; like every TestFlight build, this one stops working in December anyway. Anything after that reaches me through Feedback in "My PapaMap".
