– Fixed: when the changing-table data fails to download, PapaMap retries on its own, and the message at the top of the map is a button to try again. Check (only on a fresh install, because the app keeps its last download): delete PapaMap, turn on Airplane Mode, install it again from TestFlight and open it. Within a few seconds the header should read "Couldn't load the changing tables — try again". Turn Airplane Mode off: the pins should appear on their own within about a minute, or right away when you tap that line.
– Fixed: on a slow connection the pins appear as soon as the data lands. Check, if you have a weak signal (Edge or 3G) and a fresh install as above: open PapaMap. The header may say "Loading changing tables…" for a while, and then the pins should appear, without "Couldn't load" in between.
– Fixed: a save that takes long is no longer reported as someone else's edit. Check: answer a question on a place you know (the answer goes to OpenStreetMap). If saving fails, tap the same answer again: it should be accepted, not reported as someone else's edit; the pin changes colour and My PapaMap counts it.
– Fixed: tapping an answer twice while logged out no longer breaks the login. Check: log out in My PapaMap, tap a grey pin of a place you know and tap the right answer twice quickly. One login window should open; after you log in, the answer should be saved to the place.
– Fixed: after you switch a filter, VoiceOver stays on it. Check: with VoiceOver on, go to a filter button and double-tap it. VoiceOver should stay on that filter and read its new state, not jump to the start of the screen.
Anything unclear or broken: take a screenshot and send it through TestFlight.
