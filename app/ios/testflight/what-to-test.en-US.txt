– Fixed: when the changing-table data fails to download, PapaMap retries on its own and offers a "Try again" button. Check: turn on Airplane Mode, close PapaMap from the app switcher and open it again. After about 20 seconds the header should say it couldn't load the tables, with a "Try again" button visible on the screen. Turn Airplane Mode off: the pins should appear on their own within about a minute, or right away if you tap the button.
– Fixed: on a slow connection the pins appear as soon as the data lands. Check, if you have a weak signal (Edge or 3G): close PapaMap from the app switcher and open it again. The header may say "Loading…" for a while, and then the pins should appear, without a "Couldn't load" message in between.
– Fixed: a save that takes long is no longer reported as someone else's edit. Check: answer a question on a place you know (the answer goes to OpenStreetMap). If you ever see a message that someone else just edited the place, tap the same answer again: it should be accepted, the pin should change colour and My PapaMap should count it.
– Fixed: tapping an answer twice while logged out no longer breaks the login. Check: log out in My PapaMap, tap a grey pin of a place you know and tap the right answer twice quickly. One login window should open; after you log in, the answer should be saved to the place.
– Fixed: after you switch a filter, VoiceOver stays on it. Check: with VoiceOver on, go to a filter button and double-tap it. VoiceOver should stay on that filter and read its new state, not jump to the start of the screen.
